Embedding Things

Snow

Snow forms when water vapor in the atmosphere freezes into ice crystals that join together and fall to the ground. Each snowfall can create a quiet, sparkling landscape that transforms familiar surroundings.

Beyond its beauty, snow plays an important role in nature by insulating plants, storing water for spring, and providing a habitat for many winter animals.
